# FAQ

## Where do I find help for a specific task?

Use the table of contents on the left or **Search**. If this is your first time, start with the [Introduction](./getting-started/).

## Is this guide available in Vietnamese?

Yes. Use the language menu in the navigation bar to switch between Vietnamese and English; you stay on the page you are reading.

## I forgot my password. What now?

The app has no self-service password reset yet. Contact your organization's support team. If your account is linked to Google or Microsoft, you can still [sign in](./getting-started/sign-in) with that button.

## I didn't get the verification email

Check your Spam or Promotions folder first. If it isn't there, send it again from the sign-in screen as described in [Verify your email](./getting-started/verify-email). A link works only once and expires, so always use the newest email.

## Why is my account temporarily locked?

After too many wrong passwords in a row, the app pauses sign-in to protect your account. Wait a few minutes and try again; the lock lifts on its own. See also [Sign in](./getting-started/sign-in).

## I don't see the Google or Microsoft sign-in button

Those buttons appear only when your system has Google or Microsoft sign-in turned on. If you don't see them, sign in with your username and password, or ask your support team.

## Why does the guide send me through the app and back while I read?

The guide shares the app's sign-in session, which needs refreshing regularly (every 15 minutes by default). The app does this while it is open; if you are only reading the guide, the next page you open passes through the app to refresh the session and brings you straight back to the page you were reading. If the session has fully expired, the app asks you to [sign in](./getting-started/sign-in) again first.
