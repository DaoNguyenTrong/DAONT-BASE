# Turn on push notifications

Push notifications tell you about something new, such as being added to an organization, even when the app isn't open. Each browser and device is turned on separately.

## Before you start

- Use a browser that supports push notifications, such as a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
- Your system must have push notifications set up. If it doesn't, the tab only shows **Push notifications are not supported on this browser.**

## Steps

1. At the top right, click your avatar, then choose **Profile**.
2. Select the **Push notifications** tab.
3. Turn on the **Push notifications** switch.
4. When the browser asks whether the site may send notifications, choose **Allow**.

To turn them off, turn the switch off.

## Result

The switch is on. From now on this device receives the app's notifications even after you close the tab.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Notifications are blocked for this site in your browser settings"**: you refused the notification permission earlier, so the switch is locked. Open the browser's site settings (the padlock next to the address), allow **Notifications**, then reload the page.
- **No switch, only the "not supported" line**: the browser doesn't support it, is in private mode, or your system hasn't turned push notifications on. Try another browser or ask your support team.
