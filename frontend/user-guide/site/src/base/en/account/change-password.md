# Change your password

Change your password from time to time, or right away if you think someone else knows it.

## Before you start

- You need your current password.
- Accounts that only sign in with Google or Microsoft have no password, so they don't see the **Change password** tab.

## Steps

1. At the top right, click your avatar, then choose **Profile**.
2. Select the **Change password** tab.
3. Enter your **Current password**.
4. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters. Click the eye icon to check what you typed.
5. Click the **Change password** button.

## Result

The app shows **Password changed** and clears both fields. Use the new password the next time you sign in. Your other signed-in devices keep their sessions; to sign them out, see [Manage your sessions](./sessions).

## Troubleshooting

- **The current password is wrong**: type it again, watching Caps Lock and your keyboard layout.
- **"Changing the password is temporarily locked"**: you entered a wrong current password too many times. Wait a few minutes and try again. Signing in is not affected.
- **You forgot your current password**: the app has no self-service password reset yet. Contact your organization's support team.
