# Adjust the appearance

You can make the app easier to read and use: light or dark mode, the type and color of the menu, and the font size. These choices are remembered on the device you use.

## Steps

1. Click the **Appearance settings** button (the gear icon) on the top bar. The settings panel opens on the right.
2. Choose one or more of the options below. Changes apply at once, with nothing to save.
3. Click outside the panel or press **Esc** to close it.

| Option         | Choices                                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Color mode** | **Light**, **Dark** or **System**                                                     |
| **Menu type**  | **Static**, **Slim**, **Slim+**, **Drawer** or **Horizontal** (see below)             |
| **Menu color** | **Brand** (a purple menu) or **Transparent**                                          |
| **Font size**  | **Decrease font size**, **Increase font size** or **Reset font size** for the default |

## Menu types

- **Static**: the left sidebar shows full labels. Click **Toggle sidebar** at the top left to hide or show it.
- **Slim**: the left sidebar is narrow and shows icons only.
- **Slim+**: the left sidebar is narrow and shows each icon with a short name below it.
- **Drawer**: the left sidebar is narrow like **Slim**; point at it and it expands over the content. Click the pin button to keep it open.
- **Horizontal**: the menu runs across the top of the page.

The menu type applies on wide screens only. On small screens the menu always opens from the **Toggle sidebar** button at the top left.

## Jump to a page

Press **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K** on a Mac), or click the **Go to page…** box on the top bar. Type the name of a page or an action, then press **Enter**. Besides pages, the box offers actions such as **Switch language** and **Logout**.

## Result

The appearance changes at once and stays that way the next time you open the app in the same browser.
