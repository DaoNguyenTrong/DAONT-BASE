# Manage your sessions

Every time you sign in on a browser or device, the app starts a session. Check this list when you forgot to sign out on another computer, lost your phone, or spot a device you don't recognize.

## View your sessions

1. At the top right, click your avatar, then choose **Profile**.
2. Select the **Active sessions** tab.

Each session shows the device and browser, when it was **Last active**, its IP address and when it **Signed in**. The session on the computer you are using has the **This device** tag. The **Remember login** tag means the session survives closing the browser.

## Sign out one device

1. On the session to end, click the **Sign out** button (the arrow-out-of-door icon) on the right.
2. Click **Confirm**.

The session disappears from the list and the app shows **Device signed out**. The **This device** session has no such button; to sign out the computer you are using, see [Sign out](../getting-started/sign-out).

## Sign out every other device

1. Click **Sign out of other devices** at the top right of the tab.
2. Click **Confirm**.

Only the **This device** session remains, and the app shows **Signed out of other devices**. The button appears only when there is at least one other session.

## Troubleshooting

- **You see a device you don't recognize**: sign it out, then [change your password](./change-password) right away.
- **A device shows as "Unknown device"**: the browser didn't send device details. Use the sign-in time and IP address to recognize the session.
