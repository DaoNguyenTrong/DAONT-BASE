# Find your way around

This page introduces the fixed parts of the app and how to make it comfortable to read: light or dark theme, font size and language. These choices are remembered on the device you use.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar holds the main sections (**Home**, **Organizations**). The organization you work in, your profile and the logout button are not here; they are in the account menu (see below).

On small screens the sidebar is hidden. Click **Toggle sidebar** at the top left to open it. On wide screens you can change the menu type and color in the **Appearance settings** panel.

## The account menu

Click your avatar at the top right. The menu has:

- the list of places you can work in, to switch organization, and **Manage organizations**;
- **Profile**;
- **Language**: choose **Tiếng Việt** or **English**;
- **Logout**.

## Change the font size

1. Click the **Appearance settings** button (the gear icon).
2. Under **Font size**, choose **Increase font size** or **Decrease font size**. Click **Reset font size** to go back to the default.

Color mode and menu type are in the same panel.

## Change the language

Click your avatar, then choose **Tiếng Việt** or **English** under **Language**. All text in the app switches to the other language at once, without reloading the page.
