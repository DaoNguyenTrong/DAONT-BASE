# Find your way around

This page introduces the fixed parts of the app and how to make it comfortable to read: light or dark theme, font size and language. These choices are remembered on the device you use.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar holds the main sections (**Home**, **Organizations**). At its bottom you find:

- the organization you work in (click it to switch to another one);
- your name (click it to open your **Profile**);
- the **Logout** button.

On small screens the sidebar is hidden. Click **Toggle sidebar** at the top left to open it.

| 3   | **User guide**: opens this guide in a new tab, in the language you use |
| 4   | **Notifications**: the number on the bell counts unread notifications  |
| 5   | **Theme**: switch between light and dark                               |
| 6   | **Font size**: make the text larger or smaller, or reset it            |
| 7   | **Language**: switch between Vietnamese and English                    |

## Change the font size

1. Click the **Font size** button (the letter **A**).
2. Choose **Increase font size** or **Decrease font size**. Click **Reset font size** to go back to the default.

## Change the language

Click the **Language** button (the globe icon). All text in the app switches to the other language at once, without reloading the page.
