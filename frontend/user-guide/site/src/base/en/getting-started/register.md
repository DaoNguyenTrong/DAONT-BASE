# Create an account

If you don't have an account yet, you can create one yourself. After registering, verify your email before you sign in.

## Before you start

- An email address you can read, to receive the verification link.

## Steps

1. Open the app. On the sign-in screen, click **Don't have an account? Create one**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Username**, **Email** and **Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters.
3. Click **Create account**.

| #   | Element                                            |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Your name                                          |
| 2   | Your username, used every time you sign in         |
| 3   | The email that receives the verification link      |
| 4   | Your password, at least 8 characters               |
| 5   | The **Create account** button                      |
| 6   | Sign up quickly with a Google or Microsoft account |

## Result

The screen shows **Check your email**, and an email with a verification link is sent to the address you entered. Continue with [Verify your email](./verify-email).

## Troubleshooting

- **This username is already taken**: pick another username.
- **An account with this email already exists**: you registered before. [Sign in](./sign-in), or resend the verification email if you haven't verified it yet.
- **No Google or Microsoft button**: these buttons appear only when your administrator has set up sign-in with that account.
