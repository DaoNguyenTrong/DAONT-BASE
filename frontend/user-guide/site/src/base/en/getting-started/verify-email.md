# Verify your email

A new account can sign in only after its email has been verified. This proves the email address is yours.

## Before you start

- You have [created an account](./register) and saw the **Check your email** screen.

## Steps

1. Open the mailbox of the email address you registered with.
2. Open the verification email and click the link in it. The app opens and shows **Verifying your email…**.
3. Wait a few seconds until the app takes you to your workspace.

::: tip
The link works once and expires after a while. If it has expired, resend the verification email (see Troubleshooting).
:::

## Result

Your email is verified and you are signed in right away. From then on, [sign in](./sign-in) with your username and password.

## Troubleshooting

- **No verification email**: check your Spam or Promotions folder. If it isn't there, enter your username and password on the sign-in screen and click **Sign in**. The app shows **Please verify your email address before signing in.** and a resend form: enter your email and click **Resend verification email**.
- **This verification link is invalid or has expired.**: the link was already used or is too old. Resend the verification email as above and use the newest link.
