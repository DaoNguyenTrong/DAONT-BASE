# Sign in

Sign in to reach your workspace. Use your username and password, or a Google or Microsoft account if your system allows it.

## Before you start

- You have an account and have [verified your email](./verify-email).

## Steps

1. Open the app. The sign-in screen appears.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Keep **Keep me signed in** checked on your own computer: next time you open the app, you won't need to sign in again. Uncheck it on a shared computer.
4. Click **Sign in**.

### Sign in with Google or Microsoft

Click **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, then choose your account in the window that opens. The first time, the app creates an account for that email, or links it to the existing account with the same email.

::: info
These buttons appear only when your administrator has set up Google or Microsoft sign-in.
:::

## Result

You reach your workspace. If you opened a link before signing in, the app takes you to that page.

## Troubleshooting

- **Your username or password is incorrect.**: check them again, including capital letters.
- **This account is temporarily locked after too many failed sign-in attempts.**: wait a few minutes and try again. The lock ends by itself.
- **Please verify your email address before signing in.**: see [Verify your email](./verify-email).
