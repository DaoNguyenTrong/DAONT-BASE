# Manage members

Change someone's roles when their job changes, and remove them from the organization when they no longer work there.

## Before you start

Open the member list: click **Organizations** in the sidebar, then click the **Members** button (the two-people icon) on the organization's row. Each row is a member with their roles.

![The member list with role boxes and remove buttons](/base/images/en/organizations/manage-members.jpg)

| #   | Element                      |
| --- | ---------------------------- |
| 1   | A member's role box          |
| 2   | The **Remove member** button |

## Change roles

1. Click the member's role box.
2. Choose another role, or click the **×** on a role to drop it.

The change is saved right away and the app shows **Roles updated**. When changing roles, a member must keep at least one.

## Remove a member

1. On the member's row, click the **Remove member** button (the trash icon).
2. Click **Confirm**.

The person disappears from the list and loses access to the organization. To bring them back, [add them again](./add-member) as a new member.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Cannot remove or change the role of the last owner"**: an organization always needs at least one **Owner**. Give the **Owner** role to someone else first.
- **The role box is greyed out and there is no remove button**: you don't have the **Manage members** permission; you can only view the list.
