# Add a member

Add someone to the organization so they can work in it. The roles you choose decide what they can do there.

## Before you start

- The person must already have an account in the app. If they don't, ask them to [create one](../getting-started/register) first.
- You need the email address of their account.

## Steps

1. Click **Organizations** in the sidebar.
2. On the organization's row, click the **Members** button (the two-people icon).
3. Below the member list, enter the person's email.
4. In the box next to it, choose one or more roles.
5. Click **Add member**.

## Result

The app shows **Member added** and the person appears in the list. They get a notification that they were added to the organization.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Account not found"**: no account uses this email. Check the email, or ask the person to create an account first.
- **"This account is already a member"**: the person is already in the organization. To change their roles, see [Manage members](./manage-members).
- **"A member must have at least one role"**: choose at least one role before clicking **Add member**.
- **No add-member fields**: you don't have the **Manage members** permission in this organization.
