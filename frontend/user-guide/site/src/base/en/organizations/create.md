# Create an organization

Create an organization when your team or unit needs a shared workspace. Anyone signed in can create one, and the creator becomes its owner (the **Owner** role).

## Steps

1. Click **Organizations** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New organization** at the top right.
3. Enter the organization's **Name**.
4. Enter a **Slug**: a short name no other organization uses, made of lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only. For example `sales`.
5. Click **Save**.

## Result

The app shows **Organization created** and opens the new organization's members dialog right away, so you can [add members](./add-member). The organization comes with three system roles: **Owner**, **Admin** and **Member**. To work in it, [switch to it](./switch).

## Troubleshooting

- **The slug already exists**: another organization uses it. Choose a different slug.
