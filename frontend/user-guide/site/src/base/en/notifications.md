# Notifications

The app tells you about things that concern you, for example when you are added to an organization. New notifications appear on their own, without reloading the page.

## View notifications

1. In the top bar, click the **Notifications** button (the bell icon). The red number on the bell is how many notifications are unread.
2. The notifications panel opens with the newest at the top. It shows your 10 most recent notifications.

<figure>

![The notifications panel opened from the bell](/base/images/en/notifications/panel.jpg)

<figcaption>The notifications panel</figcaption>
</figure>

| #   | Element                                  |
| --- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Purple dot: an unread notification       |
| 2   | A notification's **Mark as read** button |
| 3   | The **Mark all as read** button          |

## Mark as read

- One notification: click the check mark on its right.
- All of them: click **Mark all as read** at the top of the panel.

The purple dot disappears and the number on the bell goes down. When nothing is unread, the number is hidden.

## Get notified even when the app is closed

Turn on **Push notifications** in your **Profile** so your device tells you even while the app is closed.
